\subsection{Kolmogorov Axioms}

\paragraph{Axiom 1: Non-Negativity}
For any event $A$ belonging to the  $\sigma-algebra \,\,\zeta$, the probability assigned to $A$ must be a non-negative real number. 

\begin{center}
    $\forall A \in\zeta,  P(A) \ge 0$ where $P:\zeta \rightarrow [0,1]$
\end{center}

\paragraph{Axiom 2: Normalization} The probability of the entire sample space $\Omega$ is exactly equal to one.

\begin{center}
    $P(\Omega) = 1$
\end{center}

\paragraph{Axiom 3: Countable of Additivity}

If $\displaystyle {\{A_n}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ is a countable sequence of disjoint events in $\zeta$, then the probability of their union is the finite sum of their individual probabilities.

\begin{center}
    $\displaystyle P\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\right) =\sum_{n=1}^\infty P\left(A_n\right)$
\end{center}
